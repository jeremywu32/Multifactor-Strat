WRDS / CRSP CIZ (Version 2) — Daily Stock Data Field Dictionary
Source table: crsp.dsf_v2
Dataset: S&P 500 Historical Daily Stock Panel

Field Name       | Description / Annotation
-----------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
permno           | CRSP Permanent Security Identifier. Unique, persistent identifier for an individual security, including across ticker changes.
permco           | CRSP Permanent Company Identifier. Unique, persistent identifier for the issuing company; one company may have multiple PERMNOs.
ticker           | Trading ticker symbol associated with the security on the observation date; may change over time.
siccd            | Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) code used to categorize the company's industry.
dlycaldt         | Daily Calendar Date. Trading date associated with the daily security observation (YYYY-MM-DD).
dlyopen          | Daily Open. First reported trading price after the regular market opens (USD per share).
dlyhigh          | Daily High. Highest reported transaction price during the regular trading session (USD per share).
dlylow           | Daily Low. Lowest reported transaction price during the regular trading session (USD per share).
dlyclose         | Daily Close. Last transaction price during the regular trading session (USD per share).
dlyprc           | Daily Price. Last regular-session transaction price; if unavailable, CRSP may use the average of closing bid and ask quotes (USD per share).
dlyret           | Daily Total Return. Daily investment return including ordinary dividend income and applicable delisting effects; expressed as a decimal ratio.
dlyretx          | Daily Price Return. Daily return excluding ordinary dividends, reflecting price appreciation and applicable non-ordinary distributions; decimal ratio.
dlyvol           | Daily Share Volume. Raw number of shares traded during the day, not split-adjusted (shares).
dlyprcvol        | Daily Price-Volume Product. DlyPrc multiplied by DlyVol; a proxy for traded dollar value, NOT actual transaction-level dollar volume (USD).
shrout           | Shares Outstanding. Total shares outstanding, reported in THOUSANDS of shares; confirm the observation's timing and adjustment basis.
dlycap           | Daily Market Capitalization. DlyPrc multiplied by shares outstanding; reported in THOUSANDS of US dollars.
dlycumfacpr      | Daily Cumulative Price Adjustment Factor. Cumulative factor used to put prices before and after split/distribution events on a comparable basis.
dlycumfacshr     | Daily Cumulative Shares/Volume Adjustment Factor. Cumulative factor used to put shares and volume before and after split-type events on a comparable basis.
dlydelflg        | Daily Delisting Flag. 'Y' indicates a delisting return is present on the observation; 'N' indicates a non-delisting return is present.

Research notes:
- Use PERMNO, not ticker, as the security-level panel key.
- Typical turnover calculation: dlyvol / (shrout * 1,000), assuming the two values refer to compatible share bases.
- dlyprcvol is NOT true dollar trading volume and must not be treated as a VWAP-derived amount.
- dlyprc and dlyclose can differ if CRSP uses a bid/ask midpoint in place of a closing trade.
- Apply corporate-action adjustments consistently before calculating price-based factors across dates.
- Missing observations and delisting flags should be handled explicitly rather than indiscriminately forward-filled.

StkDlyCumulativeAdjFactor, StkDlySecurityPrimaryData and StkDlySecurityData field definitions.
